Live latency, A/V sync and new UI. **Pre-release:** built from PR #1, not merged to `main` yet. Please test on your PC before relying on it.

**Fixes the growing live delay (~1 minute behind)**
- Stream timestamps now follow real time. Before, every frame the PC could not render in time pushed the stream further behind, permanently.
- Camera capture always uses the newest frame; it can no longer get stuck on a live camera.
- Tested on Windows CI with an injected stall: the stream timeline stayed equal to the wall clock and the delay did not grow.

**Audio / video sync**
- Automatic by default: each microphone is delayed to match the measured delay of its camera's picture.
- Manual fine-tune: −100…+100 ms in Audio → A/V sync.
- A camera's or capture card's audio is paired by device name. The first microphone is no longer grabbed, so audio is not doubled.

**Encoder and streaming**
- One encoder serves both streaming and recording at the same time (no double encoding).
- Low-latency settings for NVENC, Quick Sync, AMF and x264; B-frames off; CBR.
- Destinations: YouTube, Facebook (RTMPS), TikTok and custom RTMP. Twitch removed.
- A dead network can no longer freeze the app: connect and send have timeouts, and Stop never blocks.

**New interface**
- Dark, iOS-style layout: Input → Preview → Color → Audio → Stream.
- Status bar: VIDEO · AUDIO · COLOR · FPS · DROPPED · LATENCY · STREAM. Click it for full diagnostics: per-stage timings, app vs platform latency, CPU and RAM.

**Also included:** AI noise removal and voice enhancer; Color Studio (curves, wheels, LUT import, glow & skin, presets).

**Tip:** for the lowest viewer delay on YouTube, choose *Ultra-low latency* in YouTube Studio → Stream settings.
